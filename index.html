4. KPI Delivery Accountability (45% Total Weight)

View Goal Details & Context Description: Contribute to the overall success of the team by effectively managing assigned work, prioritising tasks, and collaborating with leaders and peers to support the achievement of agreed operational KPIs.

Measures of success:
  • Work consistently supports team KPI achievement (e.g. SLA, quality, delivery). Achieves ≥ 90% of agreed KPIs (e.g. SLA, resolution targets, quality, availability, project delivery).
  • Tasks and responsibilities completed within agreed timelines.
  • No repeated issues or behaviours that negatively impact team performance.
  • Active cooperation with leaders and peers to address workload, risks, or performance gaps.

5. Transformation (Change Adoption & Ways of Working) (10%)

View Goal Details & Context Description: Actively support and adopt changes to tools, processes, and operating models introduced as part of CBS’s transformation journey.

Measures of success:
  • Timely adoption of new platforms, tools, or processes relevant to role.
  • Participation in required training, pilots, or change activities.
  • Constructive feedback provided to improve new ways of working.
  • Demonstrates flexibility and positive attitude toward change.

6. Service Improvement, Operational Excellence & Risk Reduction (5%)

View Goal Details & Context Description: Identify, propose, and successfully implement service improvement initiatives that enhance service quality, efficiency, stability, or customer experience.

Measures of success:
  • Work is delivered in line with agreed processes and quality standards.
  • Issues, inefficiencies, or risks are raised early, not ignored.
  • Recurring problems are flagged with suggestions for improvement.
  • Errors or near‑misses are treated as learning opportunities, not hidden.
  • Daily actions support smoother, more reliable service delivery.

7. Capability Development & Knowledge Enablement (10%)

View Goal Details & Context Description: We are responsible for continuously improving our skills, staying current in our roles, and ensuring that the knowledge we gain is shared and documented so others can learn from it and services can be delivered consistently.

Measures of success:
  • Required training and role‑based learning are completed on time.
  • New skills are applied in day‑to‑day work.
  • Workarounds, fixes, and learnings are documented or shared with the team.
  • Contributions are made to KBs, runbooks, or team documentation.
  • Peers can rely on shared knowledge to resolve issues faster.

8. Information Security, Data Privacy & Policy Compliance (5%)

View Goal Details & Context Description: Ensure compliance with Information Security, Data Privacy, and internal policies through strong awareness, responsible behaviour, and adherence to required standards.

Measures of success:
  • < 10% compromise rate in end‑of‑year phishing or attack simulations.
  • Zero confirmed breaches or violations of Information Security, Data Privacy, or internal policy requirements.
  • 100% completion of mandatory compliance training, including InfoSec, Data Privacy, and other required policy training.
  • Consistent compliance with internal policies and procedures, including acceptable use, data handling, access control, and confidentiality requirements.
  • Active participation in compliance and awareness initiatives when required.
